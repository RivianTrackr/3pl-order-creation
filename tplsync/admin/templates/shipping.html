Ship Via

Each order ships the way its PO says. The PO's Ship Via is matched to the warehouse's carrier and service codes in 3PL Central, e.g. “USPS Priority Mail” becomes USPS with that service's code. A shipping account number written at the end (“UPS GRND C713X7”) is used as the order's account; words that name a service (“UPS 3DAY”) are never taken for one. If there's no confident match, the order isn't created and you get an alert.
